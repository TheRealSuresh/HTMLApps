Formula: weighted average of 14 principles (lethal ×3, serious ×2, background ×1) → 70% average + 30% worst lethal or serious score → cut by the shock absorber → plus spillover from trade partners above 4.5 → capped at 10. Bands: 7.0+ erodes first, 5.5–6.99 at risk, 4.0–5.49 muddles through, below 4.0 succeeds.

The 30 model countries use the scores from the National Erosion Index v4 (14 Oct 2026). The figures-to-score thresholds are a starting rubric, not calibrated on history. Countries you save are kept in your own account and are private to you.
